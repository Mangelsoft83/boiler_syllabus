## Process principles

Steam traps discharge condensate while limiting live-steam loss. Separators remove entrained liquid. Drains prevent water hammer and help warm steam lines during startup.

## Key engineering relationship

Condensate load ≈ Q̇_loss / h_fg for steam condensing at nearly constant pressure.

**Engineering note:** Check the units and validity range of this relationship. It is an introductory model or calculation, not a substitute for equipment manufacturer data, validated steam properties or detailed design standards.

## Dynamic behavior and modeling

Represent a trap as a condensate-dependent discharge path with optional leakage.
